About Eco Sports

Chattogram's Home For Every Sport

Eco Sports was built on a simple idea: booking a place to play shouldn't take a dozen phone calls. We run multiple turfs, courts, and a swimming pool under one roof, and we run the whole operation on real-time technology — so what you see online is exactly what's available on the ground.

Our Mission

Make quality sports facilities accessible and easy to book for everyone in Chattogram, from weekend footballers to competitive swimmers.

Real-Time, Always

Every facility, price, and offer on this site comes straight from our live system — no stale listings, no double bookings.

Built For Groups

From casual five-a-side games to corporate weekly bookings, our system supports recurring reservations and group rates.
